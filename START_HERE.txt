IMPORTANT PLATFORM REALITY
--------------------------
Android: an APK can be sent directly and installed by the recipient with Android's installation approval.

iPhone: Apple requires code signing/provisioning. A normal unsigned IPA cannot simply be sent in a messenger and installed by everyone. Use a Mac + Xcode + Apple signing, then distribute through TestFlight/App Store or registered-device Ad Hoc distribution.

"Autonomous Apps" does NOT bypass Android/iOS security. Android can list and launch user-approved apps and use official Intent/Deep-Link/API integrations. iOS does not allow a normal third-party app to enumerate/control every installed app UI.
